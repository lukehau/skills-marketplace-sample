# Review a contract

Read the agreement and surface the terms that need a human decision.

## Read the agreement
- Identify the contract type, the parties, and the effective and end dates.
- Note the governing law, term length, and renewal mechanics.

## Flag the risks
- Highlight clauses that deviate from standard terms: liability caps,
  indemnity, auto-renewal, exclusivity, and termination rights.
- Call out one-sided obligations, missing protections, or unusual penalties.
- Note anything vague or internally inconsistent.

## Summarize
- Group findings by severity: blocking, worth negotiating, and minor.
- For each, suggest a plain-language question to raise with the counterparty.
- Remind the user this is a review aid, not legal advice.
