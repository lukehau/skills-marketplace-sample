# Draft a follow-up email

Write a concise, personalized follow-up that moves the deal forward.

## Gather context
- Note who the email is to, their role, and the last interaction.
- Recall the key points, objections, and any commitments made.

## Write the email
- Open with a specific, genuine reference to the conversation.
- Restate the value in the prospect's own terms, not a generic pitch.
- Address the main objection or open question directly.
- Close with one clear next step and a proposed time.

## Keep it human
- Keep it short — a few short paragraphs the reader can skim.
- Match the tone to the relationship; avoid pushy or template-sounding lines.
- Offer to log the follow-up and set a reminder task in the CRM.
