# Build an expense report

Turn a pile of receipts and a card statement into one clean, review-ready
report.

## Gather the inputs
- Collect all receipt images or PDFs and any card or bank statement provided.
- Confirm the reporting period and the expense policy currency.

## Match and build
- Create one expense line per transaction, pairing each with its receipt.
- Match receipts to statement charges by date, merchant, and amount.
- Itemize hotel folios into room, tax, meals, and incidentals.
- Flag any charge missing a receipt or over the receipt threshold.

## Finish
- Total the report by category and highlight anything needing an explanation.
- Leave the report unsubmitted so the user can review before filing.
