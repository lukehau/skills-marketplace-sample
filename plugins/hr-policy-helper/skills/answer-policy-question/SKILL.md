# Answer a policy question

Give a clear, friendly answer grounded in the applicable policy.

## Understand the question
- Identify the topic: leave, benefits, pay, conduct, or workplace logistics.
- Note any details that change the answer, such as tenure, location, or role.

## Answer clearly
- State the policy in plain language, avoiding jargon and internal codes.
- Explain how it applies to the person's specific situation.
- Give the concrete next step: a form to file, an approval to request, or a
  team to contact.

## Stay accurate
- If the policy is unclear or varies by region, say so and point to the owner.
- Never guess at eligibility or dollar amounts; defer to the official source.
