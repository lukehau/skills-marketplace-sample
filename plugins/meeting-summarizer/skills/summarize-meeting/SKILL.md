# Summarize a meeting

Turn a transcript or rough notes into something the team can act on.

## Read the source
- Identify the meeting topic, attendees, and date if available.
- Track who said what so action items can be attributed correctly.

## Produce the summary
- Open with a two or three sentence overview of what the meeting covered.
- List the key decisions made, each stated as a clear outcome.
- List action items as owner, task, and due date where mentioned.
- Note open questions or topics deferred to a follow-up.

## Keep it skimmable
- Use short bullets, not paragraphs.
- Leave out small talk and tangents that carry no decision or action.
